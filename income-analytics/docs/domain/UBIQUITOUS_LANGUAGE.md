# Purpose

This document defines the official business terminology used throughout the Income Analytics platform.

The Ubiquitous Language ensures that developers, business stakeholders, architects, and AI agents use the same vocabulary when discussing the financial domain.

Every concept described here has exactly one meaning within the system.

---

# Core Domain Concepts

## Investor

An individual or legal entity that owns one or more investment accounts.

An Investor is the owner of financial assets but does not directly own Financial Events.

Relationships:

- Owns one or more Accounts
- Owns one or more Portfolios (logical view)

---

## Account

An investment account maintained by a financial institution.

Examples:

- XP Investimentos
- Nubank
- BTG Pactual
- Interactive Brokers
- Binance

Every Financial Event belongs to exactly one Account.

---

## Institution

A financial institution responsible for maintaining an investment account or issuing financial assets.

Examples:

- Itaú
- XP
- BTG
- BlackRock
- Tesouro Nacional

---

## Asset

A financial instrument that may generate financial events.

Examples:

- Stock
- ETF
- REIT
- Treasury Bond
- CDB
- Cryptocurrency

Assets are static reference data.

---

## Asset Type

Classification of financial assets.

Examples:

- STOCK
- ETF
- FII
- TREASURY
- CDB
- LCI
- CRYPTO

Asset Type defines business behavior.

---

## Currency

Represents the monetary denomination used by an Asset or Financial Event.

Examples:

- BRL
- USD
- EUR
- BTC

---

# Financial Domain

## Financial Ledger

The immutable historical record of every Financial Event belonging to a single Account.

The Financial Ledger is the authoritative source of financial truth.

---

## Financial Event

An immutable historical fact that occurred at a specific point in time.

Examples:

- Buy
- Sell
- Dividend
- Deposit
- Withdrawal
- Split

Financial Events are never modified.

---

## Financial Effect

The financial consequence produced by a Financial Event.

Examples:

- Position Effect
- Cash Effect
- Income Effect
- Cost Basis Effect
- Tax Effect
- FX Effect

Business Engines process Effects rather than Event Types.

---

## Event Stream

The chronological sequence of Financial Events contained in a Financial Ledger.

The Event Stream enables deterministic financial reconstruction.

---

# Portfolio Concepts

## Portfolio

A logical projection representing the consolidated financial position of one or more Accounts.

Portfolio is NOT the source of truth.

Portfolio is reconstructed from Financial Events.

Portfolio is a Read Model.

---

## Position

The quantity of an Asset owned at a specific point in time.

Position is derived.

Position is never persisted as authoritative data.

---

## Cash Balance

The amount of available money within an Account.

Cash Balance is reconstructed from Financial Events.

---

## Allocation

The distribution of investments across different categories.

Examples:

- Asset Class
- Country
- Currency
- Sector

Allocation is calculated.

---

## Snapshot

A reconstructed representation of an Account or Portfolio at a specific moment in time.

Snapshots exist only for performance optimization.

They are disposable.

---

# Performance Concepts

## Average Cost

Weighted acquisition cost of an investment position.

Average Cost is always reconstructed.

---

## Cost Basis

Total acquisition cost associated with an investment position.

Used by:

- Performance Engine
- Tax Engine

---

## Passive Income

Recurring cash generated by investments.

Examples:

- Dividends
- Interest
- Treasury Income
- FII Income
- Coupons
- Staking Rewards

---

## Realized Gain

Profit generated through completed transactions.

Example:

Asset Sale

---

## Unrealized Gain

Profit resulting from market appreciation of assets still held.

---

## Net Worth

The total value of all assets, liabilities, and available cash at a given moment.

Net Worth is reconstructed.

---

# Read Models

Read Models are optimized projections generated from Financial Events.

Examples:

- Portfolio
- Position
- Cash Flow
- Income History
- Performance
- Allocation
- Net Worth
- Monthly Summary

Read Models are disposable.

They may be regenerated at any time.

# Canonical Rules

## Rule 1

Financial Events represent facts.

---

## Rule 2

Financial Effects represent consequences.

---

## Rule 3

Financial Ledgers own Event Streams.

---

## Rule 4

Business Engines transform Events into Read Models.

---

## Rule 5

Read Models are never the source of truth.

---

## Rule 6

Portfolio is a projection.

---

## Rule 7

Snapshots are optimization artifacts.

---

## Rule 8

Derived information is always reproducible.

# Naming Guidelines

Prefer:

- Financial Event
- Financial Ledger
- Financial Effect
- Account
- Asset
- Portfolio Projection
- Snapshot
- Position
- Passive Income

Avoid:

- Transaction (too generic)
- Wallet (ambiguous)
- Balance Table
- Portfolio Entity
- Cached Position
- Stored Performance

---

# Domain Philosophy

Income Analytics is **not** a portfolio tracker.

Income Analytics is an **event-driven financial reconstruction platform**.

Financial knowledge is reconstructed from immutable historical facts.

Every calculation is:

- Deterministic
- Explainable
- Reproducible
- Auditable

# ADR-008

## Decision

Income Analytics adopts a single Ubiquitous Language across the entire platform.

Every domain concept has one official definition.

Every component, document, API, and Business Engine must use this terminology consistently.
